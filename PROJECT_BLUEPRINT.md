Blueprint/backbone phase only.

- Website status: no standalone business domain identified in lead research
- Current evidence: official Linktree is active and specifically provides WhatsApp ordering and Instagram.

## Website objective
Build a strong food-ordering/brand website around the three signature cuisine families, making the business look established without fabricating restaurant details.

## Structural backbone
1. Branded loader/logo.
2. Navigation: Home, Menu, Biryani, Mandy, Maqluba, About, Gallery, FAQ, Order/Contact.
3. Home: cinematic food hero, signature dishes, trust/reviews area when verified, WhatsApp order CTA.
4. Menu: separate Biryani, Mandy, Maqluba and verified supporting dishes. Every item gets image, short description, price only when verified, and order CTA.
5. Dish detail/recommendation section: ingredient highlights and serving information only where verified.
6. About/Story: brand philosophy and authentic-flavour positioning; no invented founder story.
7. Gallery: dish photography and brand atmosphere.
8. Ordering: WhatsApp-first flow with concise instructions and opening/lead-time information only when verified.
9. FAQ.
10. Contact/location: exact location, hours, Maps and contact once verified.
11. Footer: full identity, contact, socials, navigation and legal.

## Visual blueprint
- Direction: modern Middle Eastern/South Asian culinary editorial.
- Palette: charcoal, warm sand, saffron/earth accent, restrained metallic-like highlight.
- Typography: bold display serif or expressive sans for dish names; clean sans for utility text.
- Hero: high-contrast close-up food photography, controlled steam/light effects, layered depth.
- Menu cards: rich imagery, strong hierarchy, price/availability emphasis.
- Motion: GSAP-style section reveals, dish image parallax, subtle grain and hover depth; no distracting 3D food gimmicks.
- Mobile: sticky WhatsApp order bar, horizontal category tabs, fast image loading.

## Conversion
Primary: Order via WhatsApp. Secondary: Menu, Instagram, Directions.

## Hard rules
Never invent menu items, prices, halal/certification claims, address, hours or delivery terms. Use authentic dish imagery matching the actual dish. Content must remain easy to edit from a central data layer.
